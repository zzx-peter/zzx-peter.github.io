Zhang Zhixia 张之夏

Beijing, China

I am an undergraduate student in Computer Science and Technology at Beihang University. In Fall 2027, I will join the Discovery Intelligence Group (DIG) at Peking University as a direct-entry Ph.D. student, advised by Prof. Ling Yang.

My research interests lie in reinforcement learning, large language models, and AI agents, with a focus on how agents learn, reason, and improve through interaction and experience. My long-term goal is to build agent systems that can autonomously evolve and reliably carry out long-horizon tasks. I am particularly interested in the learning mechanisms that support these capabilities and their applications to real-world problems.
